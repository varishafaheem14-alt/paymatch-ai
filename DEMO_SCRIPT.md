# 🎬 PayMatch AI — Live Demo Click Script (5-Minute Walkthrough)

Follow this exact step-by-step guide during your live presentation or screen recording.

---

## 🏁 Preparation Before Starting
1. Open PowerShell or Terminal.
2. Run:
   ```bash
   streamlit run app.py
   ```
3. The browser will open to `http://localhost:8501`.
4. Maximize the browser window for a crisp presentation.

---

## 🖱️ Step-by-Step Demo Flow

### Step 1: Open the App (Pre-loaded Demo State)
* **What to Show:**
  * Point out the top header: **PayMatch AI — Track 4: AI Finance Controller**.
  * Highlight that **Demo Mode** has automatically loaded 45 realistic Indian transactions.
* **What to Say:**
  > *"When we open PayMatch AI, the finance team immediately sees a high-level executive health audit of their business."*

---

### Step 2: The Executive Dashboard (Tab 1)
* **Actions:**
  1. Point to the **Portfolio Health Audit** card showing health rating and **Total Amount at Risk**.
  2. Point to the KPI cards:
     * **Total Transactions:** `45`
     * **Invoiced Value:** `₹12,47,900.00`
     * **Settled Amount:** `₹10,57,476.00`
     * **Reconciliation Rate:** `~60%`
  3. Scroll slightly to show the **Reconciliation Status Donut Chart** and **Payment Channel Performance Bar Chart**.
  4. Scroll down to **High-Priority Action Items**. Click to expand the first item (e.g. `INV-2026-003` - Rohan Mehta).
* **What to Say:**
  > *"Notice transaction TXN-1003 for Rohan Mehta: The invoiced amount was ₹7,800, but only ₹7,644 was received. PayMatch AI instantly explains that the ₹156 discrepancy is a standard 2% Razorpay MDR fee deduction, and generates the exact accounting ledger entry to post."*

---

### Step 3: Transaction Audit & Search (Tab 2)
* **Actions:**
  1. Click on **Tab 2: 🔍 Transaction Audit**.
  2. In the **Filter by Status** dropdown, select **`MISMATCH`**.
     * Show that the table instantly filters to mismatch transactions with exact difference amounts in Indian Rupees (`₹`).
  3. Change status filter back to **`ALL`**, then type **`Zomato`** in the search bar.
     * Show instant fuzzy search matching `Zomato Delivery Fleet`.
  4. Under **Inspect Single Transaction Discrepancy**, pick `TXN-1005` (Ananya Iyer - Missing Payment).
     * Show the auto-generated **One-Click Resolution Outreach Template** with ready-to-send payment link!
* **What to Say:**
  > *"Finance teams can filter by any status or search any merchant. When a payment is missing, PayMatch AI doesn't just log it—it drafts a polite recovery email with a direct Razorpay payment link ready to send."*

---

### Step 4: AI Finance Assistant (Tab 3)
* **Actions:**
  1. Click on **Tab 3: 🤖 AI Finance Assistant**.
  2. Click the quick button: **`🚨 What is our biggest risk?`**
     * Show instant, detailed breakdown of the highest exposure transaction.
  3. Click the quick button: **`💳 Razorpay MDR Fee Analysis`**
     * Show the intelligent advice on distinguishing gateway fees from unpaid balances.
* **What to Say:**
  > *"Finance managers can ask any natural language query. Our built-in intelligence engine works 100% reliably with zero external API dependencies, ensuring a foolproof demonstration."*

---

### Step 5: Deep Financial Analytics (Tab 4)
* **Actions:**
  1. Click on **Tab 4: 📈 Financial Analytics**.
  2. Show the **Daily Settlement vs Billed Timeline** comparing invoiced vs settled trends over time.
  3. Show the **Top Variance Exposure by Customer** horizontal bar chart.
* **What to Say:**
  > *"This analytics tab gives controllers visual clarity on cash flow bottlenecks and identifies customers with the highest recurring variance."*

---

### Step 6: Export Reports (Tab 5)
* **Actions:**
  1. Click on **Tab 5: 📥 Export & Reports**.
  2. Click the **`⬇️ Download Reconciliation CSV Report`** button (a CSV file will download).
  3. Click the **`⬇️ Download Executive Summary Report (.md)`** button.
  4. Scroll down to show the **Live Preview of Executive Report**.
* **What to Say:**
  > *"With one click, the finance team can export the full audited dataset with AI action flags, or download a structured Executive Summary report for C-level leadership."*

---

* **What to Say:**
  > *"PayMatch AI turns 40 hours of manual reconciliation chaos into a 2-minute autonomous audit. Thank you!"*
